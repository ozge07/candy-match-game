Candy Match — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Summary

The game itself asks you for nothing. There is no account to create, no name or email is requested, and the game contains no analytics of our own.

The game shows optional rewarded ads. These are served by Unity Ads, which collects your advertising ID, your approximate location, and technical details about your device. Because the Unity Ads software starts when the game launches, this happens even if you never watch a single ad. Details below.

What the game itself does not collect

The app's own code never collects, requests, or transmits any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying information
  • GPS location, or any location permission
  • Contacts, photos, files, calendar, microphone, or camera data
  • Payment or financial information
  • In-game analytics or crash reports

What the ad software collects

Ads are served by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). The data collected goes to Unity and is used for advertising:

Collected Purpose
Advertising IDSelecting ads, avoiding showing you the same ad repeatedly, fraud prevention
Approximate location (from IP address, country/region level)Showing ads relevant to your region
In-app interactions (ad shown, watched, skipped)Ad measurement and reporting
Device technical details (model, OS version, language, network type)Showing ads compatible with your device

Blue Labs Games never sees or stores any of this; the data goes directly to Unity. Unity's own policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

You can reset or delete your advertising ID: on Android, go to Settings → Google → All services → Ads. Once deleted, apps can no longer access that ID.

Information stored on your device

So the game can remember your progress, three pieces of information are kept only in your device's own storage:

Stored Contents
High scoreA number
Unfinished gameThe layout of candies on the board, score, move count, level
Whether the tip was shownA yes/no flag

This information:

  • is kept in the app's own private area (Android SharedPreferences),
  • is never sent to any server and is not shared with other apps,
  • is deleted entirely when you uninstall the app, or via Settings → Apps → Candy Match → Storage → Clear data.

It is not personal data and does not identify you.

Permissions

Every permission the app requests comes from the ad software:

Permission Purpose
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEDownloading ads and checking connectivity
AD_IDReading the advertising ID
ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICSAndroid's ad measurement APIs
WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICEKeeping the screen on while an ad video plays
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDLetting the ad software finish pending work after the device restarts

The game itself requests no permissions: it does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or files.

Analytics

The app contains no analytics tooling or tracking technology of ours. The only data shared with third parties goes through the ad software described above.

Third-party components

The game is built with the open-source Flutter and Flame libraries. Sound effects are synthesised from scratch; no downloaded content is used. Apart from the ad software, no component collects data.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. Because the ad software collects an advertising ID, we do not present the game as being aimed at children. If you believe your child has been using this game, please write to us at the address above.

Your rights regarding your data

Because the app stores nothing that identifies you, there is no account or record for you to request the deletion of. You can delete the game progress held on your device by uninstalling the app. You can reset or delete your advertising ID through Android settings as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
